## Reading

Cleaning the span moves condition B by **+0.0025** (0.3930 cleaned against 0.3905 as script 07 used it). 
That is small enough that the preprocessing flaw does not explain the original negative result: hypothesis B fails for a reason other than dirty inputs.

Against the full paragraph, the cleaned span gives +0.0105 with a 95% interval of [-0.0134, +0.0358]. The control condition D gives -0.0170, which is what a same-length but uninformative input is worth.

The model is still character n-gram TF-IDF and cannot represent the promise-evidence relation, so condition C remains understated. That limitation is unchanged from script 07 and is what the neural rerun would address.
