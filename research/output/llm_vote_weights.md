# What an LLM vote adds, with the weight chosen out of sample

Generated by `research/scripts/22_llm_vote_weights.py`. The 601-row stratified subsample of train+val is split in half; the mixing weight is fitted on one half and the gain measured on the other, then swapped and averaged. Test is untouched.

Mixed as `(1-w) * encoder_oof + w * llm`, argmax over the result. The encoder side is out-of-fold, so it is not advantaged on these rows either.

## Reading

**The direction is empty.** Across seven configurations and four subtasks, the
honest gain is positive in two cells, both under +0.004, against a subsample of
601 rows where that is comfortably inside the noise. On `evidence_quality` --
the subtask this was meant to rescue -- **every model makes it worse**, from
-0.0046 to -0.0269.

**The earlier numbers were selection artifacts, and the size of the gap is the
lesson.** The first pass reported +0.0186 on `verification_timeline` and +0.0091
on `evidence_quality` by sweeping a weight and taking the best cell on the same
rows. Fitting that weight out of sample turns +0.0168 into **-0.0194** for
Qwen3-8B on the same field. The fitted weights say why: half A chose 0.30 and
half B chose 0.05, a factor of six apart on two halves of one sample. That is
not a weight being located, it is noise being fitted.

**Two observations survive and are worth keeping.**

*Quantisation is close to free.* Qwen3-8B in bf16 and NF4 differ by at most
0.016 across the four subtasks, and NF4 is slightly *better* on
`evidence_quality` (0.2612 against 0.2456). Sweeping a model family at 4-bit is
therefore a sound way to shop for candidates rather than a compromise.

*Scale does not help here, and the strongest model is not the best ensemble
member.* Going 8B to 14B to 32B lowers zero-shot macro-F1 on `promise_status`,
`evidence_status` and `verification_timeline`; only `evidence_quality` improves
with size (0.2456 to 0.3118). And Qwen3-32B, the best of the Qwen models on its
own, has the *lowest* complementarity with the encoder on that field --
`LLM only right` 0.126 against 0.170 for the 14B. Getting better at the task
made it agree with the encoder more, which is exactly what a vote cannot use.

This is the eighth intervention to fail on `evidence_quality`, after more
training, more data for the rare class, threshold adjustment, spans as input,
spans as a relation, dedicated heads, and augmentation.
