## Reading

**The dedicated binary head is a large gain.** A single `chinese-roberta-wwm-ext-large`
trained on nothing but Clear vs Not Clear scores 0.7104, against 0.6602 for the
entire 3-backbone x 5-fold competition ensemble on the identical subset. That is
**+0.0502** from removing the subtask from the joint four-head multi-task setup,
with far less compute.

**This corrects the interpretation of S2.** Script 08 found 14 regex features
(0.6512) matching the competition ensemble (0.6602) and I read that as evidence
that the task is largely surface-feature detection. That reading was wrong. The
right reading is that the *ensemble was underpowered on this subtask*: a properly
trained dedicated head beats the regex features by 0.0592, a clear margin. The
task has more signal in it than the competition system was extracting.

**Fusion does not help.** Stacking is worse than the transformer alone on macro-F1
(0.6994 vs 0.7104) even though it is marginally better on AUC (0.8453 vs 0.8423),
which means the stack places its threshold worse rather than ranking worse. With
`features only right` at 6.5% against `transformer only right` at 16.4%, the
encoder dominates and the regex cues are close to subsumed. S4 as a *fusion*
proposal is a dead end; S4 as a *dedicated head* proposal is the result.
