# What reason do the models give?

Generated by `research/scripts/35_explanation_probe.py`. Each of 4 frontier models judged the same paragraphs again, this time returning a one-sentence reason with the label. Two groups: the **149** rows where all four had agreed with each other and differed from the gold label, and a random **200**-row control drawn from the 621 rows where they agreed and matched it (seed 20260829).

Categories are assigned by regular expression over the reason text. The first two are the criteria `data/DATA.md` actually states; the third is the one it never mentions and that script 34 found to explain the models' consensus best. The rest are listed so the first three are not credited with everything.
