## 2. Why they fail

Two causes, both visible by finding the longest prefix of a failing piece that does occur and looking at the character where it stops.

- **the annotation skipped paragraph text** (`id 12005`): the piece stops matching after 44 characters.
  - piece continues: `推廣永續採購政策，希望由下而`
  - paragraph continues: `自 2019 年起，連續 6`
- **the annotation skipped paragraph text** (`id 12051`): the piece stops matching after 16 characters.
  - piece continues: `自身營運至2035年預計較基`
  - paragraph continues: `本公司已於2024年6月正式`
- **whitespace or a list bullet differs** (`id 12281`): the piece stops matching after 42 characters.
  - piece continues: ` 如醫師臨場服務、多元主題健`
  - paragraph continues: `如醫師臨場服務、多元主題健康`
- **whitespace or a list bullet differs** (`id 12376`): the piece stops matching after 25 characters.
  - piece continues: `  90%   的水平，範疇`
  - paragraph continues: `90% 的水平，範疇三溫室氣`

The first kind is a discontinuous annotation whose separator was not written; the second is a formatting difference between two fields of the same row. Neither is a labelling error -- the annotator marked the right text -- and both are repairable without touching the labels.

## 3. Does a greedy sequential re-alignment recover them?

## Reading

**Character F1 comparisons survive; exact match does not.** `prf` scores an empty intersection as zero whatever the prediction was, so every system loses the same rows and the ranking between systems is unaffected -- the absolute char-F1 numbers are simply depressed by roughly the empty-mask rate. Exact match is `array_equal`, so an empty gold mask matches an empty prediction: up to 114 of 1,625 promise rows and 113 of 1,335 evidence rows can be scored correct for a system that predicted nothing. Against reported exact match of 14.1% and 12.3%, that is an exposure of about half the figure, and its size is unknown until the predictions are re-scored.

**Training is affected too.** `clause_labels` reads the same mask, so on an unlocatable row the model is taught that no clause belongs to the answer. This is why the fix is not a scoring patch: the five-fold runs have to be repeated.

**The repair is cheap and works.** Greedy sequential re-alignment recovers 97.1% of promise characters and 95.8% of evidence characters on the failing rows, fully recovering 95 of 120 and 92 of 125 rows. What remains should be marked unalignable and excluded explicitly rather than silently scored as an empty answer.

**What has to be re-run**: script 32 and script 42, both fields, with the re-alignment and an explicit unalignable state, then the tagger table and the ablation table rebuilt. One GPU. Until that is done, this project's char-F1 comparisons stand and its exact-match figures do not.
