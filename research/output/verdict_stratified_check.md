# Does the disputed-versus-control contrast survive its own confound?

Generated by `research/scripts/48_verdict_stratified_check.py`. The probe set is the 349 rows a reason was generated for. Excluding rows where any of the four models failed to answer leaves 338 unanimous, split into 147 disputed and 191 control; the remaining 11 lose unanimity once the failed calls are excluded and are dropped.

## The confound, stated as a count

| group | rows | models said Clear | said Not Clear | said something else |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| control | 191 | 171 | 20 | 0 |
| disputed | 147 | 23 | 120 | 4 |

The two groups differ in their verdict mix by construction, so any rate that depends on the verdict will differ between them for that reason alone.

## Holding the verdict fixed

| stratum | reasons | cites missing figures | cites vague wording |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| control / models said Clear | 678 | 0.065 | 0.029 |
| control / models said Not Clear | 79 | 0.899 | 0.861 |
| disputed / models said Clear | 92 | 0.130 | 0.076 |
| disputed / models said Not Clear | 478 | 0.837 | 0.864 |

Within a verdict the two groups are close, and the ordering is not even consistent. **The headline contrast is a verdict-composition effect and this project does not use it.**

## The quantity that does survive

Co-occurrence: among reasons that call a paragraph vague, the share that also name missing figures. Script 49 reports it in full; this is the same stratification applied to it.

| stratum | reasons calling it vague | of those, also naming figures |
|---|---:|---:|
| control / all verdicts | 88 | 0.818 |
| control / Clear | 20 | 0.550 |
| control / Not Clear | 68 | 0.897 |
| disputed / all verdicts | 427 | 0.841 |
| disputed / Not Clear | 413 | 0.843 |

This one does not move with the stratification, which is why it and not the group contrast carries the mechanism claim.
