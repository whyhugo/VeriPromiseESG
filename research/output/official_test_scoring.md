- `evidence_quality` Macro-F1 over all four classes: **0.4642**
- Same, with `Misleading` removed from both data and averaging: **0.6194**
- A system that predicted all 3 `Misleading` rows perfectly would score 0.7142 on this task (+0.2500), worth **+0.0875 on the official total**.

So **8.75 percentage points of the official total score are decided by 3 rows out of 2,000**. For reference, the entire spread between our best and worst submission here is 0.0248.

`Not Clear` is the other weak class (F1 0.3556). And `N/A` scores only 0.6913 even though it is *deterministically implied* by `evidence_status` -- a direct symptom of predicting the four fields independently.

## 3b. Post-hoc constraint repair

A deterministic projection onto the feasible label set, applied to the existing predictions. This needs no retraining and no probabilities, so it is a *lower bound* on what proper constrained joint decoding would buy.

Rules: `promise=No` forces the other three to `N/A`; `evidence!=Yes` forces `quality=N/A`; `promise=Yes` with `timeline=N/A` takes the majority class `already`; `evidence=Yes` with `quality=N/A` takes the majority class `Clear`.
