## 5. What this does and does not settle

- The matched `within_2_years` gap is **-58.3 pp** against **-62.9 pp** for the full corpora. Matching on company retains 93% of the gap, so **company coverage explains almost none of it**.

- What remains is annotation practice, corpus construction, or a genuine change in these companies' reporting between the 2022-23 and 2024 reports. This analysis cannot separate those three.

- The `already` share is close in both corpora on the matched subset (40.3% (118/293) vs 44.8% (47/105)), so the shift is between the three real horizons rather than into or out of the already/N-A bucket.

- The promise-rate and evidence-rate gaps stay large after matching too, but those remain confounded with paragraph sampling and should not be read as claims about corporate behaviour.

- **Next step to close this**: re-annotate a sample of VeriPromiseESG4K instances under the ML-Promise guideline (or vice versa). If independent annotators reproduce the VeriPromiseESG4K distribution, annotation practice is exonerated and corpus construction is the explanation.
