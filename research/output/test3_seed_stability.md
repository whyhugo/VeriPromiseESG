## Reading

**The positive control does not hold.** Condition D -- the one that replaces the evidence with random text of the same length, and the only reason to believe this experiment can detect anything -- excludes zero on **7 of 40 seeds**. Its mean interval is [-0.0392, +0.0003], which crosses zero. The published [-0.0389, -0.0003] is one draw landing on the favourable side of a boundary the seed decides.

The other four conditions exclude zero on **no seed at all**, which is consistent with their point estimates but tells us nothing on its own: with the control failing, this design has no demonstrated sensitivity, so its nulls are uninformative rather than negative. Test 3 should be reported as a null result of undemonstrated sensitivity, not as an exclusion of the task-formalisation explanation.

**No condition predicts `Misleading` even once.** The metric is therefore a two-class macro-F1 scaled by 2/3, and every delta above is shrunk by that same constant. This does not change any sign, but it means the numbers are not comparable with a three-class macro-F1 reported elsewhere.

**What would fix it.** Three training seeds per condition and a paired test over the folds, rather than one fine-tuning run per condition with a bootstrap over test rows only. Script 42 establishes that seed variance on a comparable task is about +/-0.0066, which is a third of the effect D is trying to detect.
