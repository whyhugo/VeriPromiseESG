## Reading, and what this comparison cannot settle

The cascade is worse on every subtask and -0.0324 overall, while reaching perfect
structural consistency. But **this comparison is confounded** and should not be
read as "dedicated heads are worse". Three things differ at once between the two
rows:

1. dedicated heads vs one shared encoder with four heads
2. a single model vs a three-backbone five-fold ensemble
3. the hierarchy enforced by construction vs free prediction

Confound 2 alone could account for most of -0.0324. `research/scripts/14_joint_multitask_control.py`
removes confounds 2 and 3 by training the joint four-head model on the same
encoder, folds and seed.

What is already clean is the tighter experiment in script 12, where only
`evidence_quality` was swapped and everything else held fixed: that gave -0.0016,
and the per-class breakdown showed `N/A` improving while `Not Clear` degraded.

Note also `verification_timeline` OOF of 0.6021 against 0.6545 for the ensemble
on test -- the one subtask where a dedicated head might have been expected to
help most, given the official RAG baseline sits at 0.4227. It did not.
