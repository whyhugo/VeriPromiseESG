- English and French have text but no span annotations, so they support classification transfer only.

## 2. Chinese subset detail

- Released instances: **899** (paper Section 3.4 states 610; Table 1's Promise-Identification row totals 199).
- `promise_status=Yes`: 383 (42.6%)
- Label vocabulary differs from VeriPromiseESG4K: `Less than 2 years` / `2 to 5 years` / `More than 5 years` / `N/A`, and `Potentially Misleading` rather than `Misleading`.
- **There is no `already` class.** `N/A` covers both 'no promise' and 'already verified'. Among promise-bearing instances `N/A` is 164/383 = **42.8%**, against `already` = 44.1% in VeriPromiseESG4K -- close enough that the two classes are comparable.

| Timing (given promise=Yes) | ML-Promise ZH | VeriPromiseESG4K |
|---|---:|---:|
| within 2 years | 146 (38.1%) | 34 (2.1%) |
| 2 to 5 years | 15 (3.9%) | 498 (30.6%) |
| more than 5 years | 58 (15.1%) | 377 (23.2%) |
| N/A -- already | 164 (42.8%) | 718 (44.1%) |

Renormalised over the three real horizons: within-2-years is **66.7%** here (n=219) against **3.7%** in VeriPromiseESG4K. This reproduces the Section 1 finding on the released data rather than on transcribed percentages.
