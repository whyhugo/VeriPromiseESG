## Reading

The four systems agree with each other markedly more than any of them agrees with the gold standard, and they are unanimous on 58% of rows. On 149 of those they are unanimously wrong.

That set is not spread evenly across the labels, which is the finding: the divergence is a disagreement about a particular judgement rather than general noise. Read with the earlier result that nine interventions could not move `evidence_quality`, this points the same way -- the models are not failing to read the paragraphs, they are reading them consistently and applying a different standard of what counts as adequate evidence.

This is the material for an error analysis that does not require a better model: a set of paragraphs, already labelled by people and already judged unanimously by four frontier systems, on which the two disagree.
