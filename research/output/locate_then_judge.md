## Reading

The comparison that matters is B against D, not B against A. If handing the classifier the gold evidence span helps by about as much as handing it a random substring of the same length, the gain is from shorter input and the reformulation is not doing the work.

E and F are the deployable versions. A gap between C and F measures what the extractor costs; a gap between A and F measures what the whole locate-then-judge pipeline is worth to a system that has to find its own spans.
