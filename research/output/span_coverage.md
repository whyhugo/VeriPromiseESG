## Reading

- For `evidence_quality`, the relevant span is `evidence_string`, which is a **median 52%% of the paragraph**. Conditioning on it discards roughly half the input, not the great majority. The locate-then-judge hypothesis is therefore *plausible but not obviously decisive*, and has to be settled by the gold-span upper-bound experiment rather than argued from coverage.
- Roughly a quarter of spans cannot be recovered by exact match, and whitespace normalisation barely helps (+0.6 to +0.9 points), so the mismatch is not just spacing. Any span model needs fuzzy alignment, and the alignment failure rate is itself worth reporting.
