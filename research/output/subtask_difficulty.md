## Reading

**Macro-F1 overstates the gap.** `evidence_quality` scores 0.4642 against 0.6545 for `verification_timeline`, but 0.1547 of that difference is one class with three test rows scoring zero. Drop it and the two are 0.6189 and 0.6545 -- close.

**The gap is real on a chance-corrected scale, and it is large.** Balanced accuracy corrected for the number of classes puts `evidence_quality` at +0.289 against +0.536, +0.594 and +0.612 for the other three: **less than half of any of them**, and this measure gives the three-row class no more weight than its recall deserves.

**The failing class is `Not Clear`, F1 0.3556 on 226 rows.** Not a rare class, and not one whose definition is ambiguous in the way `Misleading`'s is. Every other field's minority class scores 0.57 to 0.70. That is the quantity the rest of this project is about.
