## Clean decomposition

The three effects that were tangled together in script 13 now separate.

**Enforcing the hierarchy costs -0.0130.** Rows 1 and 2 are the *same weights*,
differing only in whether predictions are projected onto the feasible label set.
SCR goes 86.0% to 100.0% and the score goes 0.6015 to 0.5885. Almost all of it
lands on `evidence_quality`, 0.4472 to 0.4091.

**Dedicated heads are worth +0.0176.** Rows 2 and 3 both hold the hierarchy at

**Ensembling is worth roughly +0.03.** Row 3 against row 4 is one backbone

## What this implies

The three effects are roughly additive and point at a system that has not been
built yet: dedicated heads, three backbones, and *no* hierarchy enforcement.
On these numbers that lands near 0.6061 + 0.03 + 0.013 = **0.649**, against
0.6384 for the competition ensemble.

That is a modest +0.011 and it rests on assuming additivity, so it needs to be
run rather than extrapolated. It also requires dedicated heads that each predict
their full label set including `N/A`, rather than the conditioned cascade used in
script 13, since the cascade makes consistency unavoidable.

The `verification_timeline` result is the most actionable single finding here:
a dedicated head lifts it by 0.0952 over the joint formulation at matched
consistency, and it is the subtask the official RAG baseline struggles with most
(0.4227).
