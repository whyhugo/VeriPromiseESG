# Where the headroom is

Generated by `research/scripts/17_headroom_decomposition.py` on the official 2,000-row test set, scored with the independent framework of script 16 rather than the competition formula.

## Oracle decomposition of `evidence_quality`

Each row replaces one component with the gold answer and leaves the rest of the system alone, so it upper-bounds what perfecting that component could buy.

| system | macro-F1 | gain over current |
|---|---:|---:|
| current system | 0.4642 | --- |
| + oracle gate (`evidence_status` from gold) | 0.5796 | **+0.1155** |
| + oracle Misleading (3 rows handed to the model) | 0.7145 | **+0.2503** |
| + oracle judgement (Clear/Not Clear/Misleading from gold) | 0.8597 | +0.3955 |
| perfect | 1.0000 | +0.5358 |

Reading the judgement row against the Misleading row separates them: of the +0.3955 available from a perfect judgement, **+0.2503 is the three Misleading rows alone** and only +0.1452 is everything else -- all 1,332 Clear and Not Clear decisions combined.

So the three components rank: Misleading +0.2503, gate +0.1155, the rest of the judgement +0.1452. The largest single lever is the one class that scripts 09 and 11 showed to be undetectable by any method we have.

## What limits the gate

`evidence_status` confusion matrix, rows gold, columns predicted:

| gold \ pred | N/A | No | Yes | total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **N/A** | 245 | 22 | 108 | 375 |
| **No** | 17 | 149 | 124 | 290 |
| **Yes** | 63 | 63 | 1209 | 1335 |

The gate fails in one direction: **232 rows are called `Yes` when no evidence exists** (108 with no promise at all, 124 with a promise but no evidence). Recall on `No` is only 0.514. Every one of those rows then receives a Clear or Not Clear label that cannot be right.

## Rare-class leverage across all four subtasks

| subtask | rarest class | test n | train+val n | its F1 | macro-F1 | without it | shift |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `promise_status` | `No` | 375 | 373 | 0.7024 | 0.8191 | 0.9359 | +0.1167 |
| `evidence_status` | `No` | 290 | 282 | 0.5687 | 0.7132 | 0.7855 | +0.0723 |
| `evidence_quality` | `Misleading` | 3 | 2 | 0.0000 | 0.4642 | 0.6189 | +0.1547 |
| `verification_timeline` | `within_2_years` | 44 | 34 | 0.6465 | 0.6545 | 0.6565 | +0.0020 |

Rarity alone is not the pathology. `within_2_years` has 34 training instances and the model still reaches F1 0.6465 on it, so removing it barely moves the average. `Misleading` has 2 training instances and F1 0.0000. Somewhere between 2 and 34 training examples the class becomes learnable, and only `Misleading` falls below it.

No subtask reaches two thirds of the agreement the annotators reached with each other, so there is headroom everywhere. On this scale `evidence_quality` and `verification_timeline` are close together (0.530 and 0.547), which the competition macro-F1 hides by scoring them 0.4642 and 0.6545.

## Implication for what to work on

The reachable headroom in `evidence_quality` is +0.1155 from the gate and +0.1452 from the Clear/Not Clear judgement; the remaining +0.2503 sits in three rows that nothing we have can find. Since five direct attempts on the judgement have failed, the gate is the part still worth attacking, and its failure mode is specific and learnable: recall 0.514 on `evidence_status = No`, a class with 282 training instances.
