## Reading

The tagger reaches char-F1 **0.6666** on the test set against **0.6004** for the strongest baseline on the same rows, closing 18% of the distance to the 0.9748 ceiling.

Exact match rises from 0.4% to 11.8%, which is the share of paragraphs where the predicted text is character-for-character the annotated span.

## A caveat on which metric to read

For this field the strongest baseline is "return the whole paragraph", which
scores char-F1 0.6004 by having recall 0.9154 against precision 0.4795. It
returns everything and is useless in practice, as its 0.4% exact match shows.
Character-F1 flatters it because the evidence span covers a large share of the
paragraph.

IoU and exact match separate the two properly: IoU 0.4795 -> 0.5711, exact match
0.4% -> 11.8%, a thirtyfold rise in the share of paragraphs where the returned
text is exactly the annotated evidence. On this field the +0.066 of char-F1
understates what the tagger does.
